## Install

1. Open the download link on your Android phone.
2. Open the downloaded APK from Downloads. If Android requests it, allow
   installation from the browser or Files app you used, then tap Install.
3. Open ScholarsPilot. Its HTTPS service is already configured; no PC server is
   needed. Free hosting may take about a minute to wake up on first connection.
4. Create an account with your own email. The first pilot is Bangladesh, age 13+;
   students aged 13–17 need a separate verified guardian account and approval.
5. Stay on **Verify your email to continue** until you open the email link and
   return. The app checks automatically; Check now and Resend are also available.

For an upgrade, install over your current app to preserve its records/session.
APK updates require Android confirmation; use Settings → App updates for future
versions. The guide has three optional steps and a Home exit throughout.
Already-verified users enter normally. Unverified accounts cannot access records
or the Assistant, including through an older installed version.

In Settings → Bank messages, allow SMS access, search and select a sender, then
confirm this month’s history/latest balance once. Clear new financial alerts become
records on sync. New payments ask for a voice/text purpose; earlier imported records
do not. AI can tidy the description and suggest a category. OTP/security texts are
excluded; unclear alerts need review. Raw financial SMS reaches the server for
parsing but is not stored there or sent to the model. SMS does not verify a bank
balance. Cash, manual payments, budgets and CSV history remain available. Preferred
currency reports preserve original transaction amounts. The Groq Assistant gives
stored-record explanations and general suggestions. Records are user-supplied;
the app does not transfer money or grant bank access merely by naming an account.
