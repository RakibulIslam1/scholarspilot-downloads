# Privacy and Permissions

ScholarsPilot's pilot records and explains student money. It does not hold funds, initiate payments or require bank login credentials for message/manual recording.

| Permission/data | Purpose | Boundary |
|---|---|---|
| Financial SMS | Parse supported transactions/balance statements from student-selected senders | Android grants inbox access broadly; app-side selection/filtering narrows processing. Messages can be incomplete or forged. |
| bKash notification access | Capture supported event cues from the official app | Separate opt-in; no private wallet-history API. Missing amounts require entry. |
| Microphone/speech | Turn English/Bangla spoken reasons into text | Audio persistence is disabled; the phone speech provider may process audio externally after disclosure/consent. |
| AI description/assistant context | Improve wording, suggest categories and explain relevant records | External text processing and quotas apply; review suggestions before saving. |
| Financial records | Owned account/activity/budget views and monthly planning | This is a hosted service, not wholly offline or universally end-to-end encrypted. |

Recognized OTP/security messages and promotions are filtered. Such filtering is not a guarantee against all misclassification. Permissions can be denied and manual recording remains available. Avoid entering PINs, passwords or OTPs into descriptions or chat.

Email verification and applicable guardian consent are required for the live pilot. The first audience is students in Bangladesh aged 13+; under-18 access requires guardian approval. This flow is not independent proof of age or identity, and broader safeguards remain under review.
